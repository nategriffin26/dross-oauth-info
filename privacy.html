Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

This policy covers the Google Cloud OAuth app shown as “n8n workflow” on Google’s consent screen. It is a personal, single-user app operated by Nate Griffin (“I”) for my own Gmail account. See the app description. This app is not affiliated with n8n GmbH, and I do not claim that Google has reviewed, verified, or certified it.

Whose data this is

The app is intended for one user: me. It is used by my own n8n workflows and by my personal AI assistant, Dross, to act on my own Gmail account at my direction. It does not collect data from the public, and I do not run a service for other people.

Data accessed

Through Google OAuth, these tools can access the Gmail data that the granted permissions allow. Depending on granted permissions and the task, accessed data may include message content and metadata such as senders, recipients, subjects, dates, and labels, and attachments if a task accesses them. The tools use that access to read, compose, send, and organize mail for tasks I request, including automations I configure.

How data is used

  • To carry out tasks I request for my own mailbox, including automations I configure, such as reading or summarizing mail, drafting and sending messages, and organizing mail.
  • I do not sell Google user data.
  • I do not use Google user data for advertising, and I do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

Where data and credentials are stored

  • Dross uses Executor for its OAuth connection and pyDurable as its local assistant runtime on my Mac. These tools may store credentials and local copies of data.
  • Those tools may also write local logs, task records, or other local data while they work. Depending on what a task involves, that local data can include content from Gmail.
  • n8n stores its connection details and workflow or execution data in its configured environment. This policy does not assert that n8n is hosted on my Mac.

Gmail-derived content may be retained in logs, task records, chat transcripts, summaries, and files. Retention varies by data store; this policy does not promise that content is never stored or has a single automatic expiry period.

AI providers

When I ask Dross, or a workflow that uses AI, to work with email, the relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider I have configured for that tool so that it can process it. That processing is subject to that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. I do not control, and this policy does not promise, how a third-party provider handles, retains, or uses data it receives. When I direct a message to be sent, its content is transmitted to the intended recipients through Gmail. Configured AI providers and, where applicable, services hosting my n8n environment may also process relevant data.

Retention and deletion

If pyDurable’s Gmail source cache is enabled, its refresh process removes dated cache files older than 30 days. This does not delete copies in other records. Forget can redact selected memory records but does not erase original chat transcripts or file history. Removing retained copies requires handling the relevant stores separately. n8n and AI-provider retention depend on their respective configuration and policies. Messages stored by Google remain subject to Gmail’s own deletion behavior.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked in Google Account settings. Follow Google’s account-access instructions, find “n8n workflow” under apps with access to your Google Account, and choose to remove access.

Warning: this Google Cloud OAuth app is shared with other personal OAuth clients. Revoking its access may interrupt n8n, Dross, and other clients using the same app. Removing credentials from one tool’s configured environment is a local action; it does not itself revoke Google’s authorization or delete previously obtained data.

Security

I keep credentials in the tool environments described above and limit use of this app to my own accounts and tools. No system is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This app is not directed to children and is not offered to the public.

Changes

I may update this policy as the setup changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
