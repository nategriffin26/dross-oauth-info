About this app

This site describes a Google Cloud OAuth app whose consent screen shows the name “n8n workflow”. It is a personal, single-user app run by Nate Griffin. It is not a public product or service, and it is not offered to anyone else.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by n8n GmbH, and the name is only a label on the consent screen. This site makes no claim that Google has reviewed, verified, or certified the app.

What it is for

The app is a shared OAuth client used by the owner’s own tools, so that they can act on his own Gmail account at his direction:

  • n8n, a workflow automation tool that the owner runs for himself.
  • Dross, the owner’s personal AI assistant.

What it does with Gmail

When the owner asks, these tools use the Gmail API through OAuth to:

  • read messages;
  • compose drafts and send messages;
  • organize mail, for example with labels, archiving, or moving messages.

The exact permissions requested are shown on the Google consent screen when the owner connects the account.

Who can use it

Only the owner connects his own Google account to this app. If you were not asked by Nate Griffin to approve access, do not approve it.
